UAV Trajectory Planning

Real-Time Trajectory Planning for Aerial Vehicles

BYU MAGICC Lab

Feb 2026 – Present  |  Research Assistant, Autonomous Aerial Vehicles

Developing a ROS 2-compatible C++ library for real-time trajectory planning on multirotor and fixed-wing aircraft. The planner is being tested and benchmarked in simulation, with the goal of flight testing on hardware in real-world scenarios.

  • Integrating the COAST real-time obstacle avoidance method into the ROSflight / ROScopter autopilot stack
  • Prototyped RRT-based planning with safe flight corridors (SFC) in the mavsim fixed-wing simulator and compared it against A*
GitHub: ROScopter GitHub: ROSflight pkgs GitHub: RRT/SFC

BYU FROST Lab
