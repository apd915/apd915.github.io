Current Research

COAST: Safe Flight Corridor Planner for Fast and Robust Snap-Minimized Trajectories

BYU MAGICC Lab · Advisor: Dr. Randal W. Beard

Feb 2026 – Present  |  First author, submitted to ACC 2027

An online trajectory planner for multirotor and fixed-wing UAVs that combines the mathematical guarantees of safe flight corridors with the speed of B-spline optimization.

  • C++ planner using KD-tree spatial projections and B-spline kinodynamic bounding, with sub-10 ms computation times
  • Simulated LiDAR point clouds feed an OSQP quadratic program in RViz, keeping trajectories dynamically feasible and inside continuous-space safe flight corridors
  • 2nd place nationally, SHPE 2026 undergraduate research poster competition (poster · BYU ECE news ↗)

What Transfers to Road Topology? Foundation-Model Representations Across Architectures and Domains

BYU FROST Lab · Advisor: Dr. Brady Moon

Aug 2025 – Present  |  First author, journal manuscript in preparation

A controlled empirical comparison of vision foundation models (SAM 2.1, DINOv3, and C-RADIOv3) as encoders for extracting road networks from satellite imagery.

  • Evaluated architectural augmentations and cross-domain generalization across 3,300+ satellite images worldwide
  • SAM 2.1 improves out-of-domain topological routing (APLS) by over 4 points versus prior state-of-the-art baselines
  • Built an evaluation pipeline that isolates encoder features, showing that pretraining paradigm matters more for graph connectivity than decoder complexity

Self-Clearing Channels in 3D-Printed Microfluidics

BYU Electrical Engineering · Advisors: Dr. Greg Nordin & Dr. Troy Munro

Feb 2024 – Jun 2025  |  Project lead, BYU IMMERSE presentation (May 2024)

Studied how unpolymerized resin clears out of 3D-printed microfluidic channels on its own, and how clearing time scales with channel geometry.

  • Built an electronic automation platform for device preparation, cutting prep time from 2 days to 4–6 hours and removing manual steps
  • Delivered microfluidic prints to Mechanical Engineering collaborators within 3 days of request for conference and journal work
  • Mentored two undergraduate researchers through weekly planning and accountability meetings
